When will Athlio launch?
+
We're targeting a 2026 launch. Waitlist members will get early access before the public release and be the first to build their profiles.
Is Athlio free to use?
+
Yes — joining the waitlist is completely free, with no commitment required. We'll share pricing details closer to launch.
Which sports does Athlio support?
+
We're starting with football (soccer). More sports may be added after launch based on user demand.
What devices does Athlio integrate with?
+
Athlio connects with Strava, Apple Health, and more — so your fitness data flows in automatically alongside your match logs.
I'm a coach. Is Athlio right for me?
+
Coaches can verify their players' stats and profiles, adding an extra layer of credibility that scouts trust. Coach accounts are in the roadmap.
How is my data handled?
+
Your data is stored securely and never sold to third parties. You control what's public on your profile and what stays private.
What countries will it be available in?
+
We're launching across Europe first, with a focus on markets with strong grassroots football scenes. Global expansion will follow.
